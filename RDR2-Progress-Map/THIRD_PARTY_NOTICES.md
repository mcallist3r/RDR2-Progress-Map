## Map tiles

The map tiles are **not included in this repository**. They are downloaded at
runtime from RDOMap's public CDN (`map-tiles.b-cdn.net`) by
`tools/fetch_tiles.py` and stored locally. The map imagery is copyright of
Rockstar Games and is used for personal reference only.

## Legendary animal and fish photos

The photos are **not included in this repository**. They are downloaded at
runtime from the Red Dead Wiki (Fandom) by `tools/fetch_images.py` and stored
locally. The rights to the original material belong to Rockstar Games and their
respective owners; the photos are used for personal reference only.
